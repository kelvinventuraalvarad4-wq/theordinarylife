Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

Another car picked them up during the night and took them to an abandoned house, where the coyotes were waiting. The people murmured among themselves while they were scared. Each coyote gathered their group and led them to a different van. Isai's group was the second to leave. On the way, we heard stories of women being raped, but no one dare to intervened because if they did...they could be killed. None of Isai's group went through that horrific experience.
