Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

Near the border, they slept in what appeared to be abandoned houses. Isai heard the coyote had paid the "cartel" before they arrived. The houses were infested with insects. Whenever people sat or lay on the beds, they could feel them crawling in their bodies. They were not allowed to make noise.

After several days on the road, they arrived at a house near the border. More than hundred fifty were waiting there to cross. They were saying that some of them were there for days because U.S. Customs and Borders Protection (CBP) agents were patrolling the area, and others because the coyotes had not received the rest of the money. The conditions were unsanitary. People were desperate to leave.
