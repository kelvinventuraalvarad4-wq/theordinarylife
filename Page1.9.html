Roots in the Countryside of a Distant Country

A relative of the Alvarado family lived in the middle of nowhere near a river. One day, a gang member arrived at their home asking for a glass of water. The mother gave him water. In return, he shot her in the shoulder.

Her teenage sons heard the gunshot and ran outside. When they saw their mother bleeding, they grabbed and took her inside the house. One of the brothers was shot in the leg. More armed men arrived and broke into the house. The family tried to defend themselves, and one of the attackers was killed while the others fled.

One of the brothers died after being shot several times, and the other was wounded. Neighbors arrived and carried him in a hammock toward the road, hoping to get him to a hospital. He died a few minutes away from the house.
