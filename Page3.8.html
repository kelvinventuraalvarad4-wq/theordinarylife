The Price of Dreams: The Pursuit of Higher Education

To Isai, Mrs. Contreras became like a second mom. She encouraged him to continue his higher education. Her encouragement inspired Isai to attend SUNY Old Westbury. He was happy when he found out that he had been accepted to pursue a B.A. in Hispanic Literature and Culture. Mrs. Contreras was really happy for him that she went with him to get to know the university. However, when he shared the news with his family, they said:

"College is a waste of time and money."

Instead of allowing those words to stop him, he chose to pursue his dream: earn a higher education.
