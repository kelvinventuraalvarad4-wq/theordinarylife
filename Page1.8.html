Roots in the Countryside of a Distant Country

Before he was ten years old, one of his uncles was digging a well by hand when he found a large rock. An individual told him to pour gasoline and leave there for a few days. When the well was opened and his uncle went down, the air was too strong and he fell.

The news spread quickly. Isai was at his grandma's house drinking coffee and eating bread when everyone ran shouting of what had happened. The family rushed to the house where the incident happened to help. Carlos, an 18-year-old relative and one of Isai's grandma's sons, arrived, and without a second thought, he went down without equipment and he fell too.

Isai remembers his grandmother breaking down in tears.

More than fifty people arrived from nearby neighborhoods. Authorities were called, but hours passed and no help arrived. Some people attempted to go in, but the gas was too strong.

Around noon, one of Isai's uncles was pulled out with a rope. He was still alive but died. Carlos was still in the well until evening. A person managed to bring him out with a technique that he learned somewhere. His body was injured with bruises, broken nose and bones.

Years later, another tragedy would reach Isai's family
