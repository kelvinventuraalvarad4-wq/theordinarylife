Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

Before Isai got in the car, his dad hugged him and told him something, but he doesn't recall. While he stayed behind, Isai didn't show any emotion. The driver picked up five more people: a minor, two men and a woman with a child.

That afternoon, in El Salvador, the coyote met them and three other people. They were on the road for hours until two police officers stopped the van at the Guatemala border. The coyote paid them so that they could continue their journey.
