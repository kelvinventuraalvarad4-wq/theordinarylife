Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

They crossed Guatemala in vans and buses. Never staying in the same place for more than one night. In the mountainous parts of Guatemala near Mexico, a car overturned with more than thirty people inside. People were shouting for help, and a few cars stopped to help. The car where he was, didn't stop.

After that tragedy, they arrived at a house near a lake that divides Guatemala and Mexico. They were fed alongside more than fifty people, who were hoping to reach the United States.

They crossed the lake by boat. In southern Mexico, they traveled by day in vans and buses, and slept at night in hotels or houses. The further they traveled, the more dangerous the journey became. In part of northern Mexico, three coyotes teamed up and crammed more than twenty people in the van.There was barely enough room to breathe, let alone move, and children were on their laps. They traveled like that for hours until a military vehicle chased them. The driver told them to jump into the brushes while he diverted them.
