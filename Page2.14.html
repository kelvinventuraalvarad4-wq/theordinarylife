Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

He was later transferred to another facility. It was still a detention center, but the conditions were better than the previous one. Hours and days after, he was transferred to Southwest Key Programs Inc.-Nueva Esperanza, in Texas. It is a shelter for minors who are in the process of reuniting with their families. It was like a school, as the minors attended classes, went to the play, and were allowed to speak with their family over the phone.

Two or three weeks later, he and other minors were transferred again to a camp in the middle of nowhere in New Mexico. There were thousands of minors. No matter where he looked, the only thing he saw was miles and miles of desert. During that day, the weather was hot, but cold at night.

He stayed in a "small shelter" that housed between twenty and thirty minors. Most stayed there for less than three before being reunited with their parents or siblings, but Isai was there for about a month and a half because the person responsible for him was his mom's cousin.
