The Price of Dreams: The Pursuit of Higher Education

Because he only had a work permit and did not receive financial aid, Isai worked two jobs while attending college full-time. There were moments when he was forced to decide whether to buy food on campus or put gas on his car.

He slept less than five hours and was busy most of the time; he slowly lost touch with his high school friends. While they were enjoying their young adult lives, Isai was trying to survive. He did not have much time for himself.
