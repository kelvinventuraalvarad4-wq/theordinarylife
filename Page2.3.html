Solo: Between Borders, Dangers, and Dreams

His parents woke him up between three and four in the morning. He didn't have time to say goodbye to his four siblings and grandparents. He and his dad walked to the bus station while his dad was telling him about the dangers of the journey since he had tried to cross the United States four or five times.

They arrived at rendezvous in San Miguel, El Salvador. While waiting for the coyote's assistant, his dad bought him chips and juice. The coyote is responsible for guiding the people from their country to the United States.
