The Ordinary Life of a Damned Dreamer

Isai Ventura has lived in New York since he was 13 years old. He emigrated from El Salvador to the United States on a solo journey. His story is more than just a journey, it's a story of struggles, perseverance, and triumph!

.

When Ventura was 12 years old, he left his home country with one backpack carrying two pairs of jeans, a few shirts, fifty dollars, and dreams that seemed impossible. His solo journey was psychologically difficult, to the point that he attempted to end his own life while detained in an ORR shelter. After arriving in New York, he lived a normal life. After finishing high school, he decided to pursue higher education, his family told him,"College is a waste of time and money" Instead of giving up, he worked two jobs to pay for his university tuition out of pocket. In this story, he shares his journey through three phases: childhood, adolescence, and adulthood.
